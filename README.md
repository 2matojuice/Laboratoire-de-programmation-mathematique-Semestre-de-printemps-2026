# Laboratoire de programmation mathématique

This repository contains the six lab sessions (TPs) of the *Laboratoire de programmation mathématique*, a course I took as part of the Bachelor in Computer Science at the University of Geneva (spring semester 2026).

The course applies Python to mathematical problems. The topics covered are discrete dynamical systems, probability, ordinary differential equations, prime numbers and combinatorics. Each TP is a Jupyter notebook containing the exercise statements, the solutions and their outputs. The exercise statements are taken from the course material. All notebooks are in French.

## Contents

| TP | Notebook | Topic | What's inside |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `TP1_Introduction_Python.ipynb` | Introduction to Python and its math libraries | Euclid's algorithm, the Collatz conjecture, plotting with matplotlib and NumPy, functions of two variables |
| 2 | `TP2_Systemes_dynamiques_discrets.ipynb` | Discrete dynamical systems | Newton's method, the logistic map, bifurcation diagrams, Lyapunov exponents, Julia and Mandelbrot sets, a strange attractor |
| 3 | `TP3_Probabilites.ipynb` | Probability theory | The birthday paradox, Monte Carlo estimates of π, the law of large numbers, the central limit theorem, waiting times and random walks, domino tilings and the arctic circle, Zipf's law, a Markov-chain text generator |
| 4 | `TP4_Equations_differentielles.ipynb` | Ordinary differential equations | Euler's method and how to improve it, planetary orbits around the Sun and Jupiter, exact solutions with SymPy, the double pendulum |
| 5 | `TP5_Nombres_premiers.ipynb` | Analytic number theory | The sieve of Eratosthenes, increasingly accurate ways to count prime numbers |
| 6 | `TP6_Fonctions_generatrices.ipynb` | Combinatorics with generating functions | Counting a frog's jumps, recursion, asymptotics, king's walks, integer partitions, making change with coins, DNA sequence alignment |

## Notes

- The notebooks can be read directly on GitHub, as all results and figures are saved in them.
- Running them requires Python 3 with Jupyter, NumPy, Matplotlib, SciPy, SymPy, Numba and Pillow.
- `wap.txt` contains the text of *War and Peace* and is used in TP3.
- A few illustrations from the original course material are not included in the notebooks (four in TP3, three in TP4).
